## OVERALL ARTICLE STRUCTURE NOTES

### Evidence Quality Tiers to Note Throughout

**Tier 1 (Strong):**

- Multiple independent labs
- Replicated findings
- Convergent methods
- Quantitative with statistics
- Controls appropriate

**Tier 2 (Moderate):**

- Single lab or limited replication
- One method
- Some quantification
- Basic controls

**Tier 3 (Weak):**

- Anecdotal observations
- No replication
- Qualitative only
- Missing controls
- Theoretical only without validation

### Cross-Cutting Themes to Weave Throughout

1. **Developmental stage confound** - organoids ≠ adult tissue
2. **Measurement limitations** - can't see everything
3. **Variability** - high heterogeneity
4. **Missing context** - isolated system
5. **Lack of standardization** - hard to compare studies
6. **Evidence gaps** - many unknowns remain
